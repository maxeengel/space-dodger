Privacy Policy

Astro Dodger Maxe · Last updated: 8 October 2026

Astro Dodger Maxe (“the App”) is a single-player arcade game for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Data we collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as name, email, or location. There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current version.

Data stored on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device (for example via system preferences):

  • High score
  • Language preference
  • In-game shop balance and cosmetic unlocks (virtual coins earned from score)

This data stays on your device unless you delete the App or clear its data.

Internet access

The native App is designed to play offline. The related website may load normal web resources when you visit it in a browser.

Children

The App is a casual arcade game suitable for a broad audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy if the App’s features change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer via the App Store Connect support email listed with the App, or visit the project site.
